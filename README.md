## Scrapp

Scrapp lets you turn any object in the real world into a virtual sticker. Simply put on a pair of smart glasses and say "Scrapp that." The mobile and web app allow you to view your stickers and turn them into collages. Meshy integration lets you turn any sticker into a 3D model. You can add your friends to send them stickers directly in the app or export them to your favorite platform. Hunts let you turn the locations you scrapbook into scavenger hunts for your friends.

  

Designed for Meta Adventurer glasses paired to an android smartphone.
